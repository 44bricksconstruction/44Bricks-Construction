Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

Who we are

44 BRICKS is a construction and infrastructure company operating across Ghana. This policy explains how we handle the personal information you share with us through 44bricksconstruction.com, our forms, email, and WhatsApp. We process personal data in line with Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

  • Details you enter in our forms: name, email, phone number, company, project type, location, budget range, and your message.
  • Messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical information such as your IP address and the page you submitted from, used to prevent spam and abuse.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (pages visited, device type) if analytics is enabled.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange consultations and site visits, and prepare quotations.
  • To send the confirmation email for your request and, if you subscribe, occasional project updates.
  • To keep a record of enquiries so we can follow up properly.

We do not sell your information, and we never share it with third parties for their marketing.

Third-party services

Our site embeds videos from YouTube and links to WhatsApp and Instagram. When you use them, those services may collect data under their own privacy policies. Emails are delivered through our hosting provider and email services.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting project, and for up to three years afterwards for our business records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from updates at any time. Email info@44bricksconstruction.com or call +233 543 100 015.
